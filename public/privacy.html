Botly Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Botly processes information to provide merchant storefronts, dashboard features, WhatsApp automation, customer lead management, and related support.

Information We Process

We may process merchant account details, store details, product catalog data, delivery company details, customer lead information, WhatsApp message metadata, webhook events, and technical logs required to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to run storefronts, manage orders and leads, connect WhatsApp workflows, improve reliability, prevent abuse, provide support, and comply with legal and platform requirements.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with infrastructure and integration providers needed to operate the service, including Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, Supabase, hosting providers, analytics providers, and support tools.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for service operations, security, compliance, merchant account management, or legal obligations. Merchants may request deletion using our data deletion instructions.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information processed by Botly. No online service can guarantee perfect security, but we work to reduce unauthorized access and misuse.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at botlly.bot@gmail.com.
